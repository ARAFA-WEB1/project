Ordering & Payment

How do I place an order?

Browse our medical equipment, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to provide shipping information and choose a payment method.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, PayPal, and cash on delivery for orders within Egypt. For hospitals and clinics, we also offer invoice payment options.

Is it safe to use my credit card on your website?

Yes, we use 256-bit SSL encryption to protect all payment information. We are PCI-DSS compliant and never store your complete credit card details.

Do you offer discounts for healthcare professionals?

Yes, registered doctors, nurses, and healthcare facilities receive special pricing. Contact our medical sales team for verification and pricing details.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Cairo: 1-2 business days
Major cities: 2-3 business days
Other areas: 3-5 business days
Express delivery is available for urgent medical needs.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship within Egypt. For international orders, please contact our export department at export@medicalstore.com

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order from your account dashboard.

What are the shipping costs?

Standard shipping: EGP 200
Free shipping for orders over EGP 5,000
Express delivery: Additional EGP 300
Same-day Cairo delivery: Additional EGP 600

Prescription Products

Which products require a prescription?

Medical devices such as oxygen concentrators, CPAP machines, certain surgical instruments, and medications require a valid doctor's prescription.

How do I upload a prescription?

During checkout for prescription-required items, you'll be prompted to upload a clear photo or scan of your prescription. You can also upload prescriptions from your account dashboard.

How long does prescription verification take?

Prescriptions are typically verified within 2-4 business hours during working days. For urgent medical needs, contact our medical team at +20 123 456 7890.

What makes a prescription valid?

A valid prescription must include:
- Patient name and age
- Doctor's name, signature, and stamp
- Date of issue (within last 6 months)
- Clear medication/device name and dosage
